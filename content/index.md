Performing the functions of a personal site, a blog, a wiki (but one that only [[Sylvia Nambiar|I]] update), and a place to host [[Sylvia Nambiar|my]] [[Translations/index|translations]], this site is disjointed and fragmented, bursting at the seams, shifting and slipping.

You may navigate the site with the sitemap, by jumping down your own personal hyperlinked rabbit hole, or by traversing the node graph. While navigation is best suited (in a few cases) to larger screens, reading has been optimized for all screen sizes.


(Fret not if you see a page in your name; it's either a shaved down wiki entry or a stub to which mentions of your works and/or translations refer. Feel free to let [[Sylvia Nambiar|me]] know if you'd like [[Sylvia Nambiar|me]] to make any changes (additions, removals, or modifications) to anything concerning you.)
